A plate carries a title, the name its maker uses, one sentence, two dates, a
kind, a status, and a way to reach the work. Everything after that is the
maker's note, kept exactly as written.

The line does not rank, score, verify, or summarise. It keeps where a work was
left and the words it was left with, in the order the works arrived.

This specimen stands in the instructions, not on the line. It is counted
nowhere and it answers nothing.
